# Playful / Social / Informal Decks

Use for friends, parties, classroom-light, jokes, casual storytelling, or non-business audiences.

## Principles

- Do not over-consultify.
- Make it feel alive, warm, and specific.
- Use bigger visuals and fewer words.
- Use humor only when the prompt supports it.
- Avoid sober strategy scaffolds unless the contrast is the joke.

## Style

- Expressive images.
- Strong type.
- Clear beats.
- Section slides can be punchlines.

## Avoid

- Executive dashboards.
- Dense footnotes.
- Generic KPI grids.
- Overly serious color systems.
