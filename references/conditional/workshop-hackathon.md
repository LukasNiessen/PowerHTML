# Workshop / Hackathon / Enablement Decks

Use for kickoff, working sessions, hackathons, training, enablement, and team rituals.

## Principles

- Make the group action obvious.
- Include ownership and working model.
- Show what happens after the presentation.
- Keep instructions short and operational.
- Use question / pause slides intentionally.

## Useful Slides

- Agenda
- Where we are
- Way of working
- Ownership model
- Goals
- Rules of engagement
- Hackathon instructions
- Demo / share-out

## Hackathon Slide Pattern

- What to work on
- What “done” means
- Timebox
- End state: demo, PR, launch, promotion, or decision

## Facilitation

- Question slides can be just a single question mark.
- Leave room for the presenter to explain.
- Make the action feel energizing, not bureaucratic.
